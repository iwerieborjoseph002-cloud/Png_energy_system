# Contributing to the PNG Energy System Project

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the PNG (Pure Natural Gas) Energy System Project.

This repository is a structured engineering research framework focused on fuel conversion, atomization, combustion, and system-level energy analysis.

---

## 1. Project Focus Areas

This repository focuses on the following core research modules:

- Fuel atomization research
- Catalyst process optimization
- Fuel injection dynamics
- Combustion stability and knock analysis
- Sensor and atomization alert systems
- Advanced process optimization
- Engineering documentation and system architecture

---

## 2. Contribution Principles

All contributions must follow these principles:

- Maintain technical and scientific clarity
- Use structured engineering writing style
- Support claims with logical or referenced backing
- Preserve modular system architecture
- Avoid unsupported assumptions or vague statements
- Ensure consistency across all modules
