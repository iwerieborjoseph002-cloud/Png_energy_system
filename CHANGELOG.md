# [v2.0.0] — Engineering Expansion Framework

**Status:** Released

Version 2.0.0 expands the PNG Energy System from its foundational modular architecture into a seven-module integrated engineering framework.

## Major Changes

### 1. Catalyst Process Optimization in the PNG Energy System

Established the catalytic-processing layer of the PNG Energy System.

Key areas include:

- Fe-Ni catalytic reactions
- Catalyst composition and performance
- Temperature and pressure optimization
- Hydrocarbon formation
- Reaction control
- Catalytic process optimization
- Fuel-conversion performance

---

### 2. Fuel Injection Dynamics

Established the fuel-delivery and injection layer.

Key areas include:

- Injector nozzle flow behavior
- Pressure-based fuel dispersion
- Injection dynamics
- Fuel-delivery characteristics
- Spray formation timing
- Interaction between injection and atomization

---

### 3. Fuel Atomization in Internal Combustion Engines

Established the fuel-atomization layer for internal combustion engine applications.

Key areas include:

- Spray angle
- Droplet formation
- Droplet characteristics
- Air-fuel mixing behavior
- Atomization quality
- Combustion-efficiency impact
- Fuel spray behavior
- PNG fuel performance considerations

---

### 4. Combustion Stability and Knock Analysis

Established the combustion-analysis layer.

Key areas include:

- Combustion stability
- Knock formation mechanisms
- Flame propagation behavior
- Pressure-wave dynamics
- Combustion-performance analysis
- Interaction between fuel preparation and combustion behavior

---

### 5. Sensor and Atomization Alert System

Established the monitoring and diagnostic layer.

Key areas include:

- Fuel-injection monitoring
- Atomization-quality assessment
- Sensor-based condition monitoring
- Combustion-stability alerts
- Fault-detection concepts
- Engine-performance feedback

---

### 6. Expansion Layer (System-Level Scaling and Future Architecture)

Established the system-level scaling and future-architecture layer.

Key areas include:

- System-level scaling
- Integration with external energy systems
- Future system architectures
- Conceptual hybrid architecture modelling
- System expansion pathways
- Emission-reduction considerations
- Future subsystem integration

---

### 7. Advanced Process Optimization (Intelligent System Control and Performance Optimization)

Established the intelligent control and advanced optimization layer.

Key areas include:

- Intelligent system control
- System-wide performance optimization
- Integrated fuel-to-energy performance
- Energy-loss analysis
- Combustion-inefficiency analysis
- Process optimization
- Performance monitoring and feedback
- Long-term operational performance assessment

---

## System Architecture

Version 2.0.0 establishes the following engineering progression:

**Catalyst Process Optimization**  
↓  
**Fuel Injection Dynamics**  
↓  
**Fuel Atomization in Internal Combustion Engines**  
↓  
**Combustion Stability and Knock Analysis**  
↓  
**Sensor and Atomization Alert System**  
↓  
**Expansion Layer - System-Level Scaling and Future Architecture**  
↓  
**Advanced Process Optimization - Intelligent System Control and Performance Optimization**
