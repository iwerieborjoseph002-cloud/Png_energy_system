# PNG Energy System v2.0.0 – Engineering Expansion Framework

## 1. Overview

The PNG (Pure Natural Gas) Energy System v2.0.0 represents the engineering expansion phase of the PNG Energy System project.

Building upon the conceptual modular architecture established in v1.1.0, this version focuses on the development of detailed engineering descriptions, mathematical relationships, system interactions, and process-level analysis across all system modules.

The objective of v2.0.0 is to transform the conceptual framework into a structured engineering model capable of supporting future simulation, validation, and optimization studies.

---

## 2. System Scope

The PNG Energy System models the conversion of reactive carbon and steam into hydrocarbon fuels through thermochemical and catalytic processes, followed by injection, atomization, combustion, monitoring, and optimization stages.

The system is organized into seven interconnected engineering modules:

1. Fuel Atomization
2. Catalyst Process Optimization
3. Fuel Injection Dynamics
4. Combustion Stability and Knock Analysis
5. Sensor and Atomization Alert System
6. System-Level Expansion Framework
7. Advanced Process Optimization

---

## 3. Engineering Development Objectives

The primary objectives of v2.0.0 are:

- Expand all modules into detailed engineering documents.
- Establish governing equations and technical relationships.
- Define inter-module dependencies.
- Develop system-level performance analysis methods.
- Formalize optimization pathways.
- Prepare the framework for future simulation studies.

---

## 4. System Foundation

### 4.1 Syngas Generation

Reactive carbon reacts with steam at elevated temperature to generate synthesis gas (syngas).

### 4.2 Catalytic Conversion

The generated syngas serves as the feedstock for catalytic hydrocarbon production.

### 4.3 Fuel Utilization Pathway

Produced fuel progresses through:

Catalytic Conversion → Fuel Injection → Fuel Atomization → Combustion Dynamics → Energy Output → Monitoring & Optimization Feedback

## 6. System Integration Strategy

Each module shall be developed as an independent engineering document while maintaining compatibility with the overall PNG Energy System architecture.

Inter-module interactions shall be defined through process dependencies, energy flow relationships, and feedback mechanisms.

### 6.1 System Interpretation Note

The PNG Energy System is not strictly linear. It is a layered control architecture that combines physical fuel conversion processes with monitoring, expansion, and optimization functions.

The system may be interpreted as four interacting engineering layers:

- C1–C4: Physical fuel and combustion domain
- C5: Observability and sensor feedback layer
- C6: System expansion and integration layer
- C7: Global optimization and performance regulation layer

While the modules can be studied individually, they operate as an interconnected engineering framework in which information, energy, and performance feedback propagate across multiple system levels. This layered interpretation provides the foundation for future simulation, validation, and optimization activities planned for PNG Energy System v3.0.0.
